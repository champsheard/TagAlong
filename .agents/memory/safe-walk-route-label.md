---
name: Tagalong route naming
description: Naming for the lighting-informed walking-route option.
---

**Rule:** The user wants the lighting-informed walking-route option labeled “Safest.” Keep clear caveats that its score is based on available lighting data and is not a guarantee of safety.

**Why:** “Safest” is the requested product wording, while the route score is a limited lighting-data estimate and does not measure every factor affecting safety.

**How to apply:** Use “Safest” consistently in app UI, demos, documentation, and tests. State the estimate basis and retain the no-safety-guarantee disclaimer.
